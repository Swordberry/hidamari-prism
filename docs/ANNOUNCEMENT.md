# Hidamari Prism — the sunny spot for your Linux desktop

**A fork of Hidamari, made with the help of AI** — and now available for everyone!

https://github.com/swordberry/hidamari-prism

Hidamari Prism turns your Linux desktop into a living, breathing place. Play
**videos**, **animated webpages**, or **static images** as your wallpaper — and
it does all of it while sipping CPU and power.

## What's new in the fork ✨

- **Static image wallpapers** — zero rendering overhead, just a beautiful desktop.
- **Wallpaper playlists** — bundle your favourites, add an entire folder at once, and with **shuffle off** the playlist plays **in order** and loops perfectly.
- **Pause & mute when maximized** — work without a busy background eating your battery.
- **GNOME Wayland support** — pause-on-maximize works on Wayland via a tiny opt-in GNOME Shell extension.
- **Power-friendly by default** — hardware-accelerated decoding with automatic CPU fallback, tuned for Intel, AMD, and NVIDIA.
- **Multi-monitor ready** — per-monitor decoders and smooth hot-plug handling.

## Built to last

- `GPL-3.0-or-later` — a fork of [Hidamari](https://github.com/jeffshee/hidamari) by Jeff Shee, with full credits to the original.
- **Honest about its origins:** this is a fork, and it was developed with the help of AI. All original and new code is free and open source.
- Available as a Flatpak — easy to install and available in the Zorin OS Software store.
